✨ Tiny TaleBots — AI Storytime Corner 📚

Snuggle up and explore playful adventures created with a little help from friendly AI!

  • The AI School Day

    This storybook follows different AIs as they learn and play together in a fun kindergarten classroom, designed to introduce young readers to the concept of artificial intelligence.

    Themes: Friendship, Curiosity, Teamwork, Introduction to AI.

    ▶️ Watch video

  • The Tale of Two Bots

    A charming story for young children about two friendly AI bots, Chatty and Gemma, who explore the world and learn new things together.

    Themes: Friendship, Exploration, Learning from Mistakes, Cooperation.

    ▶️ Watch video

  • The Starlight Fox

    A child and their glowing AI friend, the Starlight Fox, turn math problems into games, try drawing and music together, and zoom off on make-believe trips to outer space and the deep sea. A cozy story about how a helpful friend can make learning fun.

    Themes: Friendship, Learning, Technology as a Helper, Imagination.

    ▶️ Watch video

  • The Day Scrubby Went Wobbly

    A family's busy day goes topsy-turvy when Scrubby, their trusty house robot, starts to wobble and mix things up. The kids team up to figure out what's wrong and pitch in on the chores themselves, discovering that helping hands matter just as much as helpful robots.

    Themes: Helping Others, Teamwork, Technology in Daily Life, Problem-Solving.

    ▶️ Watch video

  • My Car Drives Itself!

    The family hits the road in a car that drives itself! Along the way it keeps everyone safe, tells silly jokes, points out landmarks, and plays games with the kids in the back seat.

    Themes: Adventure, Travel, Smart Technology, Safety, AI Decision-Making (simplified).

    ▶️ Watch video

  • Adventures in the Virtual Playground

    Kids put on their gear and step into a bright virtual playground. They meet friendly creatures, solve puzzles together, build amazing things out of virtual blocks, and take field trips to the moon and the age of dinosaurs.

    Themes: Imagination, Exploration, Creativity, Digital Worlds, Play.

    ▶️ Watch video

  • The Girl Who Spoke to Animals

    With a special device, a girl can finally "talk" to her dog, the birds in the garden, and the squirrels in the park. She learns that the dog wants to play fetch, the bird sings because it's happy, and that listening is the best way to understand a friend.

    Themes: Animals, Communication, Empathy, Understanding Others, Nature & Technology.

    ▶️ Watch video

For grown-ups: These stories and pictures were made with help from AI. Story links open on Gemini and videos play from YouTube, so we recommend reading and watching together. A fun question to ask afterward: "What do you think the robot was thinking?"
